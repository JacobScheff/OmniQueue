OmniQueue (bundle app.omniqueue) v1.0 — Guideline 2.1 notes

WHAT IT IS / WHO IT IS FOR
Independent Disneyland (Anaheim) day planner. Guests set ride priorities and must-dos, pick where they are standing, and get a short next-ride plan. The problem it solves: choosing the next ride from live waits, walk time, remaining park time, and personal taste. Target audience: park guests. Not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company.

NO LOGIN, IAP, UGC, OR PERMISSION PROMPTS
- No accounts, registration, login, or account deletion. Sign-In Required = No. No demo username/password.
- No paid content, In-App Purchase, or subscriptions.
- No user-generated content, reporting, or blocking.
- No prompts for Location, Contacts, Camera, Photos, Microphone, or App Tracking Transparency. "Location" is a manual land/ride picker (no GPS). Guest prefs stay in on-device UserDefaults. Privacy nutrition labels: no data collected.

HOW TO USE (fresh install; Wi-Fi or cellular required)
1. Launch the app. A boot screen fetches the wait board, then a first-launch disclaimer appears. Tap "Got it — plan my day".
2. Plan: shows UP NEXT, "Why this pick?", Refresh waits, and Your route (Now / Next / Then). Pull to refresh or tap Refresh waits.
3. Tap any route stop, then tap an alternative to pin it; the rest of the plan rewrites around that pin. Use Clear if a pin banner appears.
4. Rides: search, sort (My priority / Shortest wait / A-Z), set Skip/Low/Medium/High (or Advanced 0-100), Must-do, and Rode it.
5. Me: Current stop (pick a land or ride), arrival/leave times, Undo/Redo, Clear data (local reset only — this is the closest analog to account deletion). About links: Website, Privacy, Support.
No sample files. No hidden menus. If waits fail to load, tap Refresh waits; overnight most rides may show CLOSED and the planner still runs on that board.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- ThemeParks.wiki public API (https://api.themeparks.wiki/v1/entity/7340550b-c14d-4def-80bb-acdb51d49a66/live) for unofficial standby waits. In-app credit: "Wait times powered by ThemeParks.wiki". Their terms (https://www.themeparks.wiki/terms) allow commercial wait-time / planner apps with that credit; no park affiliation is implied. No API key required for this endpoint.
- Microsoft ONNX Runtime (bundled) runs the routing model fully on-device. License linked on Me.
No OmniQueue server, authentication, ads, analytics, or payment processor.

REGIONS
Features and content are the same in every region. Catalog is Disneyland Anaheim only. Live waits are that park's current board regardless of reviewer locale. English UI only.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry. No Disney license: attraction names appear only to identify publicly known rides. In-app disclaimer states we are not a Disney product and waits are unofficial estimates, not official park data. ThemeParks.wiki use is permitted under their public API terms with on-screen attribution. No tickets, Lightning Lane, copyrighted media, or park credentials.
